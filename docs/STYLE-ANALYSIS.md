# Style Analysis — reference Shorts

Date: 2026-11-27 | PC: Office
Sources: `3-Idiots-2009-SnapYT.App.mp4` (143 s), `The-48-Laws-SnapYT.App.mp4` (116 s), both
360x640 / 30 fps / H.264 ~594 kbps / AAC stereo 48 kHz. Measured only; nothing copied.

## Pacing and camera

- 18 hard cuts in the 143 s video, 10 in the 116 s video (one 5-shot burst at 15.3-16.4 s).
- The rest is slow motion on static art: 5-10% zoom per shot (Ken Burns) plus small pans.
- Audio never falls below -32 dB for 0.4 s → narration over a continuous music bed, no silent gaps.

## Characters

- Flat vector, no face outlines, soft edge shading, head ~0.35 of body height, 3-tone skin shading.
- Story A: young woman (long black braid, gold hoops, pink kameez + mint dupatta, tears), mother
  (green saree), Lamia (blue saree, yellow blouse, glasses, bun); extras are flat silhouettes.
- Story B: elderly father (white beard, cap, glasses, white panjabi + brown shawl), Kamal
  (mustache, maroon panjabi, gold chain), young man in a light blue shirt.

## Text

- Watermark "Fahad Bhuiyan" top-left on every frame.
- Narration caption bottom-centre: black rounded pill, bold white Bengali, key words in yellow.
- Title chip top-centre: red or green rounded rectangle with drop shadow, one line.

## Motion graphics (recurring templates)

- WhatsApp chat mockup (green header, voice-note waveform, sticker, Bangla Gboard, status bar).
- Calendar card, analog wall clock, official result table with red stamp, laptop video call,
